Privacy Policy for BD Exam Result

Last updated: 29 October 2026

BD Exam Result ("the app") is provided by Mini Island Studio ("we", "us"). Contact: dazzlecartsllc@gmail.com. Package name: com.minigameisland.bdresult.

1. Summary

The app helps you check exam results of the education boards of Bangladesh. We do not create accounts and we do not run a server that stores your roll number, registration number or result. The app shows ads from Google AdMob and can send push notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging.

2. Information you enter

Board, roll number, registration number and exam year. The app uses them only to get your result. They are sent to the result website you use (for example an education board result portal) over an internet connection, at your request. If you choose to save a result or turn on a result alert, the details are stored on your phone only. We do not receive them.

3. Information collected automatically by third parties

Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) may collect your app set ID, IP address (for approximate location) and, where available on your device, your advertising ID, together with ad interactions and diagnostic data, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Firebase Cloud Messaging uses an installation ID and push token to deliver notifications.

The app also downloads a small settings file (notices, result date, update messages) from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). GitHub can see your IP address, like any website you visit.

These services have their own privacy policies: policies.google.com/privacy and GitHub Privacy Statement. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or change personalised ads settings, in your Android settings. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads and offers ad privacy options in Settings.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows result alerts and messages we send. You can turn them off in the app settings or in Android settings at any time.

5. SMS

The "Check by SMS" button opens your own messaging app with a ready message. The app does not read, receive or send SMS by itself. Your mobile operator may charge for the message.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Storage, deletion and security

Saved results, settings and alerts are stored on your phone. You can delete them in Settings > Delete data, or by uninstalling the app. Network connections to result websites use HTTPS where the website supports it. No system is perfectly secure.

8. Links to other websites

The app opens links to third-party websites (education boards, universities, news sites). Their privacy practices are their own.

9. Independent app

BD Exam Result is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or representing the Government of Bangladesh, the Ministry of Education or any education board.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version. Continued use after a change means you accept it.
